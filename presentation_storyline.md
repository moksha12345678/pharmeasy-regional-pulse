
# Presentation Storyline

## 1. For an Executive — Situation, Complication, Resolution

### Situation
Regional sales were reviewed across April-June 2026, and Guntur sales increased from ₹62,442.27 in April to ₹138,738.93 in May.

### Complication
The Guntur April-to-May sales movement was +122.19%, which exceeded the 8% significance threshold and represents a material month-over-month movement. The following month, Guntur sales decreased by 28.11% to ₹99,745.18, so the increase should not be treated as a confirmed sustained trend.

### Resolution
Review the Guntur movement through the dashboard's regional trend, category breakdown, and monthly detail before taking further action. Validate the underlying business context and monitor the next monthly result before treating any explanation as confirmed.
