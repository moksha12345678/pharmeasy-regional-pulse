
# Reliability Checklist

1. Safety check: Confirm that the report contains no unsupported external claims or sensitive information before proceeding.

2. Validation: Verify that all reported metrics and flagged regions match the computed Part 2 results.

3. Critique/refine: Review the generated CII blocks and memo for unsupported conclusions, unclear wording, and required risk tags, then refine them as needed.

4. Human sign-off: Require a human reviewer to select approve, edit, or reject through `review_gate_v1()` before external use.
